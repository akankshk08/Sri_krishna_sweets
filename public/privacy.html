What this website stores

Your cart (product, weight and quantity) is saved in your own browser's local storage so it is still there after a refresh. The checkout form keeps a draft of your details in this browser tab only, and it is cleared when the tab is closed. This website has no user accounts and no database. It does not send your details to our servers.

Ordering on WhatsApp

When you press "Place Order on WhatsApp", your name, mobile number, delivery address, instructions and order are put into a WhatsApp message on your device. Nothing is shared until you press Send in WhatsApp. After that, we use these details only to confirm, prepare and deliver your order and to contact you about it. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through WhatsApp.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Product photos are served from this website.

Your choices

You can clear your cart at any time from the cart, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. To ask us to delete order details we hold, message us on WhatsApp or email us.
